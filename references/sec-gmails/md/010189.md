                        Dear John Wooten,

                       Over the past week, the Review and Selection Panel of the Stellar Community Fund reviewed numerous submissions, but unfortunately your submission did not meet the requirements ([Link 2](<https://stellarcommunityfund.gitbook.io/scf-handbook/scf-awards/official-rules-for-submissions>)) set by the selection panel at this time. 

                      We will close your submission for now, but you'll be able to enter SCF with a new submission two rounds from now (SCF#25 - Submission Deadline is March 10th, 2024) 

                      Please find feedback on your submission from panel reviewers below.

                                    Reviewer commented at 06.03.2024 22:16:

                                    "

  Product & Ecosystem Value

- 
    There is a lot of information but not much related to what SCF reviewers care about: the Stellar integration. They haven't improved their submission based on the feedback from last time:

- 
    Unclear use of Stellar/ops mainly around Stock tokenization to allow for these benefits and the regulation implications.

- 
    Many of the deliverables aren't directly tied to the Stellar integration.

- 
    Unclear what the traction looks like, are the 11 + 8 accounts for testing?

- 
    Last submission asked to focus on the Soroban part, but they've totally removed that.

- 
    It's unclear if they're waiting for SEC approval or if they're approved, and for what exactly. As a last add-on, the designs need work to get customers.

  Technical Architecture

- 
    The thesis included, while thorough, doesn't explain how the technical architecture of how this project uses Stellar. Section 6 only mentions Stellar as a technology to look into: "We looked at a number of the Decentralized Exchange protocols currently available, and chose two of them to analyze more closely: The 0x Protocol and Stellar’s native decentralized exchange. Both of these protocols allow for the exchange of various assets without requiring a centralized authority to process them."

- 
    Github shows no sign of Stellar integration either. There is however some explanation on what they have done: "Regarding our inaugural asset, we currently have a modest but highly relevant group of 11 investor accounts on Stellar. Off-chain, we have 8 legacy accounts in the process of onboarding. These accounts hold claimable balances, set to expire this year in alignment with federal securities laws."

  Deliverable and Budget

- 
    Budget looks strange. It contains a deliverable "Blog Post Collection" and "Innnovative Free courses" that aren't directly tied to the Stellar integration and development and therefore not eligible under the current SCF budget guidelines.

- 
    The deliverable of Primitive Mobile App isn't necessarily related to the Stellar integration, but it's the only development deliverable.

  Team and Experience

- 
    Team is just one person, who isn't a core developer. To be able to make this happen, there likely need to be additional developers.

"
