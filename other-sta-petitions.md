# 2006-16

1. July 28, 2009 — STA Notice of Intent to Submit a Petition for Commission Review. Edward L. Pittman of Dechert, for the Securities Transfer Association, gives notice under Rule 430 that STA intends to seek Commission review of the June 30 delegated approval. The document is captioned "SR-DTC-2006-16 – Notice of Intent to Submit A Petition for Commission Review." It was received by the SEC on July 31. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2009/staintentnotice072809.pdf)
   [SEC PDF](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2009/staintentnotice072809.pdf)

2. August 4, 2009 — STA Petition for Commission Review. This is your PDF. STA formally asks the Commission under Rule 430 to review and set aside the Division of Trading and Markets' delegated approval. Its caption is Release No. 34-60196 / File No. SR-DTC-2006-16. SEC stamped it received August 5. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2009/stapetition080409.pdf)
   [SEC petition PDF](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/other/2009/stapetition080409.pdf)

3. October 15, 2009 — DTC Response to STA's Petition for Review. DTC, through Proskauer Rose, expressly responds to the August petition and uses Release No. 34-60196; File No. SR-DTC-2006-16. It says STA filed the petition on August 4 and asks the Commission to deny it. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/dtc/2009/dtcresponse101509.pdf)
   [SEC PDF — DTC response](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/dtc/2009/dtcresponse101509.pdf)

4. November 20, 2009 — STA Reply of Petitioner. STA answers DTC's October 15 response. Again, the caption is Release No. 34-60196 / File No. SR-DTC-2006-16. The reply expressly recounts that STA filed "the Petition" on August 4, 2009 under Rule 430. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/dtc/2009/stareply112009.pdf)
   [SEC PDF — STA reply](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/dtc/2009/stareply112009.pdf)

5. February 23, 2010 — DTC Supplemental Response. This is particularly useful because DTC says it submitted the supplemental response at Commission staff's request, addressing STA's argument under Exchange Act § 17A(b)(3)(H). It is still captioned File No. SR-DTC-2006-16, with no separate petition docket. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/dtc/2010/dtcsupp022310.pdf)
   [SEC PDF — supplemental response](https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/dtc/2010/dtcsupp022310.pdf)

6. March 12, 2010 — Office of the Chairman memorandum. This memorializes a February 12, 2010 meeting with Chairman Mary Schapiro and STA representatives. The memo is addressed simply "TO: File No. SR-DTC-2006-16" and says the meeting discussed "the issues discussed in the STA's Petition for Review." This may be the strongest evidence that the Commission itself treated the petition as part of the existing SRO file rather than opening a `3-xxxxx` proceeding. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/comments/sr-dtc-2006-16/dtc200616-49.pdf)
   [SEC PDF — Chairman meeting memorandum](https://www.sec.gov/comments/sr-dtc-2006-16/dtc200616-49.pdf)

The SEC's current DTC rulemaking index itself confirms this organization: under 34-60196 / SR-DTC-2006-16, it places the Supplemental Response, Reply of Petitioner, DTC's Petition response, STA Petition, and STA Notice of Intent together as "See Also" materials. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/self-regulatory-organization-rulemaking/dtc?field_display_title_value=\&file_number=\&month=All\&order=field_publish_date\&page=15\&release_number=\&sort=asc\&year=All)
[SEC SR-DTC-2006-16 docket page](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/self-regulatory-organization-rulemaking/sr-dtc-2006-16)

There is also a later explicit SEC citation: Commissioner Luis Aguilar's February 21, 2014 statement, _Addressing Known Risks to Better Protect Investors_, footnote 43, cites the document as "Petition of Securities Transfer Association, Inc. for the Commission to review and set aside DTC Rule SR-DTC-2006-16 … 2009 LEXIS 3043 (Aug. 4, 2009)." That is where the `2009 LEXIS 3043` identifier shows up; it is a database citation, not an SEC proceeding number. [SEC](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/speeches-statements/2014-spch020421laa)
