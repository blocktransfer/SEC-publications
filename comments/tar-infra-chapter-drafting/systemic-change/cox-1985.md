## Proxy

end of 8:

> Earlier this month, the Commission adopted new Rule 14a-13 and modifications to Rule l4b-1(c)

Rule 14a-13 is still the core current rule on the issuer/registrant side of that system.

The modern framework is basically:

* Rule 14a-13(a): if the issuer knows its voting securities are held of record by a broker, bank, voting trustee, etc., it must inquire through those record holders about the beneficial owners and determine how many copies of the proxy materials/annual report are needed. The rule expressly says that where the registered holder is a clearing agency such as Cede & Co. for DTC, the issuer must inquire through the clearing agency and then its participants. ([Customs Mobile][1])
* Rule 14a-13(a)(4)-(5): the issuer supplies the intermediary with the materials needed for the beneficial owners and, on request, pays the intermediary's "reasonable expenses" of sending them onward. So yes, this is the present regulatory basis for passing the proxy-distribution/mailing expense back to the issuer. ([Legal Information Institute][2])
* Rule 14a-13(b): the issuer can request the names, addresses, and positions of beneficial owners who consented or did not object to disclosure - basically the NOBO-information mechanism - and again must reimburse the reasonable direct and indirect expense of producing that information. ([Legal Information Institute][2])
* Rule 14b-1 is the matching broker/dealer-side obligation. The broker reports the approximate number of beneficial owners, forwards proxy materials to them, and, when requested, supplies the issuer with the names/addresses/positions of non-objecting beneficial owners. It generally has seven business days to answer the initial 14a-13 inquiry and five business days after receipt to forward the materials. ([Legal Information Institute][3])
* Rule 14b-2 does essentially the corresponding job for banks and respondent-bank chains. It requires the bank to identify the number of beneficial-owner customers, pass proxy materials downstream, and provide consenting/non-objecting beneficial-owner information. ([Legal Information Institute][4])

The actual broker/bank forwarding duties are in 14b-1 and 14b-2.

And yes, the current rule is unusually explicit about DTC: if the holder of record is Cede & Co., the issuer is supposed to go through DTC and then the DTC participants. ([Legal Information Institute][5])
