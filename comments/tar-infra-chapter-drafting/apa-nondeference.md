## Theoretical setup

A meaningful opportunity to participate depends on the practical conditions under which a person can prepare a response. The availability of a submission process does not, by itself, explain the time and effort required to use it. My starting point is the relationship between the opportunity to respond and the physical work of drafting that response.

That relationship provides the setup for explaining my position toward APA deadlines. Before addressing the timing of my submissions, I need to explain how I produce them.

## TAD responsibilities and investor access

That institutional responsibility also bears directly on the operating framework for a TAD. A TAD operates with substantially more responsibilities than a conventional transfer agent (TA). It would be unreasonable to allow all transfer agents to give their investors access to a decentralized exchange, such as the Stellar Decentralized Exchange (SDEX),[^sdex-access] without appropriate, comprehensively developed operating protection schemes. The framework should address those additional responsibilities and the protections needed when investors gain access to decentralized trading.
