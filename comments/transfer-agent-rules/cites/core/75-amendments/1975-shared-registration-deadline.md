Clearing agencies and transfer agents entered the federal §17A registration regime together, with the same December 1, 1975 registration deadline.

Source: [SEC, 41st Annual Report (1975)](https://www.sec.gov/files/1975.pdf), p. 5. On that page, immediately after discussing §17A clearing-agency and transfer-agent regulation, the Commission notes that §12(m) directed it to study securities registered in “street name” and report its conclusions to Congress.
