# Whistler Investments, Inc. v. Depository Trust & Clearing Corp.  539 F.3d 1159 • No. 06-16088 • Argued 10 March 2008

First, the district court was wrong about field preemption.

Congress had not excluded states entirely from regulating securities clearance and settlement. The court specifically discussed Section 17A(d)(4) and 17A(f)(3).


Second, the dismissal was nevertheless correct under conflict preemption.

The court held that state-law claims targeting the operation or existence of the federally approved Stock Borrow Program would interfere with the federal regulatory framework.
