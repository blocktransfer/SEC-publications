# NanoPierce Technologies Nevada Supreme Court, 20 September 2007–168 P.3d 73

The court rejected field preemption. It relied on three provisions:
- Section 28(a) of the Exchange Act, 15 USC 78bb(a), preserving state remedies that do not conflict with federal law.
- Section 17A(d)(4), expressly preserving nonconflicting state regulatory authority.
- Section 17A(f)(1) and (3), demonstrating Congress's willingness to permit specified state differences from certain Commission rules.
