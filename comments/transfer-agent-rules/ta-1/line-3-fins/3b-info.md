> "Financial Industry Number Standard" ("FINS" number) is a six-digit number assigned by Omgeo, an affiliate of Depository Trust Company, to organizations engaged in securities transactions. Registrants may determine if their institution has a FINS number by telephoning Omgeo Product Operations at (212) 855-2264 or (toll-free) at (877) 664-3625. Registrants that do not have a FINS number may obtain one free of charge by writing to Omgeo, Product Operations, 55 Water Street – 51st Floor, New York, New York 10041, or sending a FAX to Omgeo, Product Operations, at (212) 855-5445. When requesting a FINS number, use the registrant organization's letterhead paper for the letter or FAX (there is no form). Provide the registrant organization's name, address, and type of business. Be sure to include a signature at the bottom of the request letter or FAX. If you also include your return E-Mail address, Omgeo will provide an E-Mail notification of the newly-assigned FINS Num.
