

**89 FR 8467 (February 7, 2024)**

An SEC approval order explicitly describes DTC's $1.9 billion bank credit facility and states that DTC may pledge securities constituting collateral of a defaulting participant to obtain settlement financing.

This is considerably more direct evidence of DTC's modern collateral arrangements than the 1993 Regulation U opinions.

The relevant authority is **Exchange Act Release 34-99456, at 8467**. [GovInfo](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-02-07/pdf/2024-02420.pdf)
