# Bank- and SEC-regulated transfer agents: disclosed historical observations (1970–2026)

Research note for [ARA non-deference](README.md). This collects **only historical dates for which an identified primary source actually discloses usable counts or proportions**. It is not an interpolated annual time series.

**Measure:** A transfer agent's *appropriate regulatory agency* (ARA), not whether a transfer agent is ultimately bank-owned. The bank ARA category covers the OCC, Federal Reserve Board, and FDIC. Totals and percentage estimates are attributed to the dates and populations used in each cited publication, rather than silently harmonized.

## Federal Register citations and primary-source links

Each Federal Register PDF link opens the **full daily issue**, with `#page=` targeting the PDF's numbered page containing the cited material; this PDF page index differs from the printed FR page number.

1. **1975 registration framework — 40 FR 51182–84 (Nov. 4, 1975).** The SEC adopted Rule 17Ac2-1 and uniform Form TA-1 under the post-1975 division of responsibility among the SEC and bank agencies. [Full daily issue, p. 51182](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1975-11-04/pdf/FR-1975-11-04.pdf#page=14). **No 1970 registration breakdown** can be taken from this adoption notice.
2. **1998 original proposal — 63 FR 12062–63 (Mar. 12, 1998).** Footnote 5 on p. 12063 discloses approximately **1,360 total** and **740 for which the SEC is ARA**. [Full daily issue, p. 12063](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1998-03-12/pdf/FR-1998-03-12.pdf#page=86).
3. **1998 final rule — 63 FR 37688 (July 13, 1998), n.5.** Repeats approximately **1,360 registered** and **740 SEC-ARA**. [Full daily issue, p. 37688](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1998-07-13/pdf/FR-1998-07-13.pdf#page=221).
4. **1999 Y2K operational capability final rule — 64 FR 42012 (Aug. 3, 1999).** Economic/regulatory flexibility analysis estimates **1,120 registered TAs**, **600 non-bank TAs**; the estimated 520 bank TAs are derived. [Full daily issue, p. 42012](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1999-08-03/pdf/FR-1999-08-03.pdf#page=21). 
5. **2015 historical retrospective — 80 FR 81959–60, 81968 (Dec. 31, 2015).** Says *most* pre-1975 TAs were banks or trusts and estimates **approximately 95 registered TAs that are banks or bank subsidiaries** as of that release. This is a **different descriptive classification**, with **no matching total disclosed in the cited passage**; do not add “95” as a bank-ARA-share data point. [Full daily issue, p. 81959](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2015-12-31/pdf/FR-2015-12-31.pdf#page=227) and [p. 81968](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2015-12-31/pdf/FR-2015-12-31.pdf#page=236).
6. **2022 snapshot, published 2023 — 88 FR 20616, 20677 n.7 (Apr. 6, 2023).** **335 Commission-registered; 67 banking-agency-registered.** [Full daily issue, p. 20677](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2023-04-06/pdf/FR-2023-04-06.pdf#page=301).
7. **2023 snapshot, published 2024 — 89 FR 47746 (June 3, 2024), § IV.C.3.e.** **251 Commission-registered; 64 banking-agency-registered; 315 total.** The final rule draws on data as of Sept. 30, 2023. [Full daily issue, p. 47746](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-06-03/pdf/FR-2024-06-03.pdf#page=315).
8. **2026 snapshot — 91 FR 57005 (Sept. 4, 2026), Table 4.** **272 SEC; 24 OCC; 21 Federal Reserve; 10 FDIC; 327 total.** [Full daily issue, p. 57005](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-04/pdf/FR-2026-09-04.pdf#page=271).

## Other primary sources that supply disclosed years (not Federal Register counts)

- **1976: SEC 1976 Annual Report**, transfer-agent registration discussion: approximately **2,400 total** registered with the SEC and federal bank regulators following implementation. [SEC annual report](https://www.sec.gov/files/1976.pdf).
- **1976: OCC 1976 Annual Report**, fiduciary activities section: **947** national banks registered as TAs at the report's year-end. [OCC report reproduced by Federal Reserve Bank of St. Louis](https://fraser.stlouisfed.org/title/annual-report-comptroller-currency-56/1976-19198/fulltext).
- **1976: FDIC 1976 Annual Report**, administration section: **444** insured state nonmember banks registered as TAs as of Dec. 31, 1976. [FDIC annual report](https://www.fdic.gov/system/files/2024-07/fdic-ar-1976.pdf).
- **2013: SEC National Examination Program Examination Priorities**, Transfer Agent Program: approximately **460** TAs, approximately **75% SEC-registered** and the remainder bank-registered. [SEC priorities](https://www.sec.gov/files/national-examination-program-priorities-2013.pdf).
- **2012–2025: SEC historical data visualization**, an additional agency-produced series not transcribed here; individual annual points must be read from the underlying SEC download before being added. [SEC annual TA-by-ARA series](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/statistics-data-visualizations/transfer-agents/number-sec-registered-bank-registered-tas-2012-2025).

## Limits for citation or charting

- Do **not** portray 1976's 58% ratio as an audited exact share: the SEC and banking-agency sources are not a single synchronized census, and Federal Reserve registrants are not included in the identified bank count.
- Do **not** infer a value for 1970, 2014, 2017, 2025, or any other absent year merely from nearby points or an earlier approximate sketch.
- Do **not** equate **bank registration** with **bank ownership**. A subsidiary, holding-company relationship, charter, and statutory ARA assignment require separate analysis.
- The 2015 “95 banks or subsidiaries” description is preserved as a standalone historical observation, **not** a percentage-series row.
- Treat SEC figures described as “approximately” or “estimated” as estimates even where the arithmetic gives a precise-looking percentage.
