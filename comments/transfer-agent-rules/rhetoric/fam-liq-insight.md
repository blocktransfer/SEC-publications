### DTCC Series D preferred stock

DTCC's own Fixed Rate Reset Non-Cumulative Perpetual Preferred Stock, Series D is the example here, not an outside issuer's stock.

- On June 8, 2021, DTCC issued 2,000 Series D shares with $0.50 par value and a $250,000 liquidation preference per share, for $500 million aggregate liquidation preference. DTCC reported $9.1 million of issuance costs and $490.9 million of net proceeds.
- Dividends were fixed at 3.375% per year through June 20, 2026. From June 20, 2026 onward, the rate resets to the five-year U.S. Treasury rate plus 2.606%.
- Arnold & Porter describes the transaction as a $500 million Rule 144A preferred-stock offering that closed June 15, 2021.

Sources:
- DTCC Q2 2021 financial statements (initial issuance terms): https://www.dtcc.com/Globals/PDFs/2021/August/05/DTCC-Financial-Statements-Q2-2021
- DTCC 2021 annual financial statements: https://www.dtcc.com/-/media/Files/Downloads/legal/financials/2021/DTCC-Annual-Financial-Statements-2021.pdf
- DTCC 2025 annual financial statements (still outstanding at Dec. 31, 2025): https://www.dtcc.com/-/media/Files/Downloads/legal/financials/2025/DTCC-Annual-Financial-Statement-2025.pdf
- DTCC Q1 2026 financial statements (pre-reset terms immediately before June 2026 reset): https://www.dtcc.com/-/media/Files/Downloads/legal/financials/2026/DTCC-Q1-2026-Financial-Statements.pdf
- Arnold & Porter transaction announcement: https://www.arnoldporter.com/en/perspectives/news/2021/06/arnold-and-porter-advises-dtcc


If the relevant 5-year Treasury rate at the June 2026 reset was around 4.19%, then the new Series D dividend rate would be roughly:
4.19% + 2.606% = 6.796%

So it would jump from 3.375% to about 6.8% — basically double. Treasury’s June 18, 2026 5-year par yield was 4.19%. [U.S. Department of the Treasury](https://home.treasury.gov/resource-center/data-chart-center/interest-rates/TextView?field_tdr_date_value=202606\&type=daily_treasury_yield_curve)

On the $500 million liquidation preference, that would imply roughly:

- old annual dividend: $16.875 million
- at ~6.796%: about $33.98 million annually

DTCC’s filings confirm the Series D switched from the fixed 3.375% rate after June 20, 2026 to five-year Treasury + 2.606%. [DTCC](https://www.dtcc.com/-/media/Files/Downloads/legal/financials/2026/DTCC-Q1-2026-Financial-Statements.pdf)

# Conclusion

Using only public filings, we have identified about 0.8–1.1% of each DTC bond issue by name. This likely understates the actual proportion held by institutions with public reporting (some fund holdings may be double-counted across sources). The vast majority remains in unknown hands given the limits of TRACE/Bloomberg disclosures. Closing the gap would require privileged data (e.g. full dealer reports or DTC participant breakdowns). Still, our report demonstrates the methodology for leveraging TRACE and filings to trace bond ownership to a surprising degree.
