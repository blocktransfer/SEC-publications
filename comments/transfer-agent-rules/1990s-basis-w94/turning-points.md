Yes. There was **real SEC transfer-agent work product in the late 1990s**, just not, from what I can find, a publicly released draft/finalization document explicitly carrying **S7-35-94 / 59 FR 63662** forward.

The strongest examples are:

* **Feb. 5, 1997 — 62 FR 6469.** Although principally a broker-dealer electronic-recordkeeping rule, the **Division of Market Regulation expressly extended a no-action position to transfer agents and clearing agencies**, allowing electronic storage for records required under Rules **17Ad-6, 17Ad-10, 17Ad-11, 17Ad-13, and 17Ad-15**. The SEC's 1997 Annual Report describes this as applying to transfer agents. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][1])
* **1997 — Lost Securityholders rules.** The SEC adopted substantive new/amended TA rules establishing minimum search requirements for lost securityholders. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][2])
* **1998 — Y2K transfer-agent work.** The SEC proposed and adopted **Rule 17Ad-18/Form TA-Y2K**, and conducted a substantial TA examination program. Its later report says it examined **83 nonbank TAs in 1996–97**, then conducted **101 on-site Y2K reviews in 1998**. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][3])
* **Aug. 1998 — S7-25-98 / 63 FR 47209.** A genuine TA proposed rule amending **Rule 17Ad-14**, including requiring reorganization agents to establish depository accounts and expanding book-entry processing. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][4])
* **March/May 1999 — S7-11-99 / 64 FR 15310.** SEC proposed a major revision of **Form TA-2 and Rule 17Ac2-2**, specifically to improve Commission oversight of the transfer-agent industry. That proposal even added DRS information to TA-2. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][5])
* **May 25, 1999 — S7-17-99 / 64 FR 29608.** SEC proposed rewriting **Rule 17Ad-7** to expressly permit electronic and micrographic record storage. This became final in 2001. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][6])

So **the Division was plainly actively producing TA regulatory work in 1997–99 while AG24/S7-35-94 remained listed at Final Rule Stage**.

## 2001 adoption of electronic and micrographic recordkeeping (S7-17-99)

The S7-17-99 recordkeeping amendments reached a final rule independently of the broader S7-35-94 transfer-agent modernization proposal. The rulemaking sequence, in chronological order, is:

1. **May 25, 1999 — proposed rule (published June 2, 1999):** Release **34-41442**, File **S7-17-99**, **64 FR 29608–29614**. The Commission proposed expanding Rule 17Ad-7 to permit electronic and micrographic preservation of the records required under Rule 17Ad-6, subject to safeguards for integrity, indexing, duplication, and regulatory access. [Official Federal Register edition (GovInfo)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-1999-06-02/pdf/FR-1999-06-02.pdf).
2. **April 27, 2001 — final rule (published May 1, 2001; effective May 31, 2001):** Release **34-44227**, File **S7-17-99**, **66 FR 21648–21660**. The Commission adopted amendments allowing registered transfer agents to preserve required records on electronic media, microfilm, or microfiche. [Commission release](https://www.sec.gov/rules-regulations/2001/04/recordkeeping-requirements-transfer-agents); [official Federal Register edition (GovInfo), beginning at page 21648](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2001-05-01/pdf/FR-2001-05-01.pdf#page=25); [Federal Register document page](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2001/05/01/01-11005/recordkeeping-requirements-for-transfer-agents).

**What the 2001 rule changed.** For transfer agents choosing electronic or micrographic retention, amended Rule 17Ad-7(f) prescribed controls for record security, integrity, and recovery; indexes and separately kept duplicates; audit trails; timely production of records for examination; and escrow of the information needed to access electronic records. It also provided for undertakings by third-party records custodians. Transfer agents remaining with paper records were not required by these amendments to change their recordkeeping methods. The final rule completed **S7-17-99**, **not S7-35-94**.
