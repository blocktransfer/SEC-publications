# Atkins calendar — 19 Aug. 2025

Potentially relevant background for the transfer-agent proposal. Do not characterize these meetings as concerning the proposal absent additional evidence.

## 19 Aug. 2025

Chairman Paul Atkins's public calendar lists:

- 11:05 a.m. — Speaking engagement at the Wyoming Blockchain Symposium.
- 1:00 p.m. — Meeting with Securitize co-founder Carlos Domingo; BNY Managing Director Patrick Corker; BNY Chief Product and Innovation Officer Carolyn Weinberg; Anchorage co-founder Nathan McCauley; Figment co-founder Lorien Gabel; Wormhole co-founder Robinson Burkey; and Forge CEO Kelly Rodrigues.
- 3:00 p.m. — Meeting with ERC3643 President Dennis O’Connell and Daniel Coheur, Head of Digital Assets and Fund Distribution.
- 3:30 p.m. — Meeting with Kraken co-CEOs Arjun Sethi and Dave Ripley and Senior Manager Andriana VanderGriend.

## Why flag it

- Securitize LLC was an SEC-registered transfer agent. Its 2025 TA-1/A identifies the SEC as its appropriate regulatory agency and gives FINS 003641.
- Atkins had specifically identified ERC3643 in his 31 July 2025 “American Leadership in the Digital Finance Revolution” speech as an example of a token standard incorporating compliance features.
- The same afternoon therefore contains a notable cluster involving a registered transfer agent/tokenization firm and a token-compliance-standard group. It is worth cross-checking against later rulemaking records, meeting memoranda, emails, and FOIA productions.
