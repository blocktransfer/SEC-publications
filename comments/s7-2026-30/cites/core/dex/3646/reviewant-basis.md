§11A(e) (15 U.S.C. 78k–1(e), as added 1975)

>summarized here
authorized the SEC "to make a study of the extent to which persons excluded from the definitions of 'broker' and 'dealer' maintain accounts on behalf of public customers for buying and selling securities registered under section 12 of this title", and whether those exclusions are consistent with investor protection and the Act's purposes.
