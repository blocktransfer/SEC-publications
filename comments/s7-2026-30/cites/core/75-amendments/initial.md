## no evidence that the recommended brochure was produced

SEC annual reports and releases from that era (1977–1980) describe new proxy-forwarding rules (e.g. Exchange Act Rule 14b-1) but make no mention of a customer brochure. Similarly, historical NASD and exchange archives have no record of a jointly-branded "street name" brochure

## later notices

investor education on this topic has later been handled by other means. For example, the SEC (and FINRA) have issued investor bulletins on holding securities (e.g. "Holding Your Securities – Get the Facts," 2003,1 and a joint SEC/FINRA "Holding Your Securities" bulletin in 202323). A FINRA "Know the Facts" page on Direct Registration (2023) covers similar ground.45 None of these later publications were issued jointly by the national exchanges and NASD.
