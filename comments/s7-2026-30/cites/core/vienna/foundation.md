## and hence Berliner Kassenverein

Germany developed a closely related model

Berliner Kassenverein operated securities-giro custody in the late nineteenth century, followed by Kassenvereine at other German exchange centers

The practice received an explicit statutory foundation in the German Depotgesetz of 1937

https://pure.mpg.de/pubman/item/item_3641166_1/component/file_3641167/globaler-effektenhandel-complete.pdf

```
Berlin       Frankfurt       Hamburg       Munich       etc.
   |             |              |             |
Kassenverein  Kassenverein   Kassenverein  Kassenverein
```
That is an important difference from the later American path. Europe did not immediately have one giant national securities depository. It often had exchange-city/regional depositories.

In 1989, the regional securities clearing/deposit banks in Berlin, Düsseldorf, Frankfurt, Hamburg, Hanover, Munich and Stuttgart were merged into Deutscher Kassenverein (DKV)   ⟶     Deutsche Börse Clearing
