# 2008 DRS Profile remuneration

SR-DTC-2008-07; Exchange Act Release No. 34-58910; 73 FR 68465, 68466-67 (Nov. 18, 2008)

Page mapping: page 4 of the SEC release corresponds to Federal Register page 68466; the ongoing-payment schedule continues onto 68467.

Key point: the approved rule made broker-dealers fund transfer-agent development and ongoing operation of the DRS Profile enhancements through DTC. Broker-dealers were required to fund 75% of the system costs, capped at $200,000 per transfer agent, and DTC was the conduit that collected and distributed that remuneration. DTC then made the one-time payments to eligible transfer agents and charged broker-dealers a $1.00 surcharge per Profile transaction for up to 24 months to recover the up-front remuneration. 73 FR 68466.

For ongoing support, DTC also charged broker-dealers $0.75 per Profile transaction, with the fee adjusted annually for DRS Profile volume. DTC paid eligible transfer agents $2,080 per month for 2,000 or more monthly Profile transactions, $800 per month for 200-1,999 monthly transactions, and nothing below 200 monthly transactions; the transfer agents represented on the DRS Ad Hoc Committee agreed that transactional remuneration would not exceed $25,000 per transfer agent per year. 73 FR 68466-67.

DTC's current fee schedule still charges $0.75 per DRS Profile DO and rebates those collections to qualifying transfer agents under substantially the same monthly tiers approved in 2008.
