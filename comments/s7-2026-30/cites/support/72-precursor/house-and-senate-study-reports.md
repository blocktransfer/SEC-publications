# 1972 Securities Industry Study — House and Senate reports

Direct report links for the two separate 1972 congressional Securities Industry Study reports.

## House report

**Subcommittee on Commerce and Finance, House Committee on Interstate and Foreign Commerce, _Securities Industry Study_, H.R. Rep. No. 92-1519, 92d Cong., 2d Sess. (1972).**

Direct full-text Google Play Books copy:

https://play.google.com/store/books/details/United_States_Congress_House_Committee_on_Intersta?id=3c9EAQAAMAAJ

Library/catalog backup:

https://pegasus.law.columbia.edu/record/1600509

Notes:

- 170 pages.
- This is the House's own study/report, separate from the Senate study.
- The report was formally filed as **H. Rept. 92-1519**.
- Later SEC releases cite it as the "House Study."

## Senate report

**Committee on Banking, Housing, and Urban Affairs / Subcommittee on Securities, _Securities Industry Study_ (for the period ended February 4, 1972, pursuant to S. Res. 109), 92d Cong., 2d Sess. (Comm. Print 1972).**

Direct full-text Google Play Books copy:

https://play.google.com/store/books/details/United_States_Congress_Senate_Committee_on_Banking?id=-cvGnVahTfIC

Library/catalog backup:

https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB27877477

Notes:

- 88 pages.
- This is the **committee report / committee print**, not a hearing transcript.
- It is the source cited elsewhere in this repo for the CCS / Cede & Co. discussion at p. 14.
- The existing `senate-securities-industry-study-1972.md` had only a Google Play collection link; the direct report URL above is the missing direct link.

## Do not confuse with the later 1973 Senate study

The Senate issued a larger later report in 1973:

https://www.govinfo.gov/app/details/CPRT-93SPRT88033

That 1973 report is a separate document and should not be substituted for the February 1972 committee print when citing the earlier study.
