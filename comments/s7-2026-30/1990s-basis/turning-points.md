Yes. There was **real SEC transfer-agent work product in the late 1990s**, just not, from what I can find, a publicly released draft/finalization document explicitly carrying **S7-35-94 / 34-35040** forward.

The strongest examples are:

* **Feb. 5, 1997 — Release 34-38245.** Although principally a broker-dealer electronic-recordkeeping rule, the **Division of Market Regulation expressly extended a no-action position to transfer agents and clearing agencies**, allowing electronic storage for records required under Rules **17Ad-6, 17Ad-10, 17Ad-11, 17Ad-13, and 17Ad-15**. The SEC's 1997 Annual Report describes this as applying to transfer agents. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][1])
* **1997 — Lost Securityholders rules.** The SEC adopted substantive new/amended TA rules establishing minimum search requirements for lost securityholders. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][2])
* **1998 — Y2K transfer-agent work.** The SEC proposed and adopted **Rule 17Ad-18/Form TA-Y2K**, and conducted a substantial TA examination program. Its later report says it examined **83 nonbank TAs in 1996–97**, then conducted **101 on-site Y2K reviews in 1998**. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][3])
* **Aug. 1998 — S7-25-98 / Release 34-40386.** A genuine TA proposed rule amending **Rule 17Ad-14**, including requiring reorganization agents to establish depository accounts and expanding book-entry processing. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][4])
* **March/May 1999 — S7-11-99 / Release 34-41204.** SEC proposed a major revision of **Form TA-2 and Rule 17Ac2-2**, specifically to improve Commission oversight of the transfer-agent industry. That proposal even added DRS information to TA-2. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][5])
* **May 25, 1999 — S7-17-99 / Release 34-41442.** SEC proposed rewriting **Rule 17Ad-7** to expressly permit electronic and micrographic record storage. This became final in 2001. ([Securities and Exchange Commission][6])

So **the Division was plainly actively producing TA regulatory work in 1997–99 while AG24/S7-35-94 remained listed at Final Rule Stage**.
