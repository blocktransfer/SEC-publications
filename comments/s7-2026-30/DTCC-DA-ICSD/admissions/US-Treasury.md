TRADES Commentary, 31 CFR Part 357 Appendix B:
> A Security Entitlement is the interest that an Entitlement Holder has in a Treasury book-entry security.
>
> Treasury → Federal Reserve Bank → Participant → Broker-Dealer → Individual Holder
and says the Participant, broker-dealer, and individual holder all have security entitlements at their respective intermediary level. Most importantly, Treasury says:
"A Participant's interest in a Treasury book-entry security is a Securities Entitlement."
