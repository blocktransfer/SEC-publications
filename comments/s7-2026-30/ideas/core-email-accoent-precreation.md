Not in the **statute itself**. Section 17A of the Exchange Act, 15 USC 78q-1, gives the SEC the broad mandate to establish linked clearance/settlement facilities and facilitate transfers of record ownership; it does not literally say “an investor may DRS without first opening an account at the transfer agent.” ([Legal Information Institute][1])

The original 1996 DTC DRS filing describes a DTC participant instructing a transfer agent **“to establish a DRS account for a shareholder,”** after which the transfer agent sends the shareholder a transaction advice confirming that the account has been established. ([govinfo.gov][2])


Current DTC documentation remains consistent with that architecture: a participant submits a **DRS Withdrawal**, which removes the beneficial owner’s shares from the participant’s DTC position and credits them to the customer’s account at the transfer agent. ([DTCC Learning Center][4])
