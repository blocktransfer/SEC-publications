## What the amendment did

DTC filed SR-DTC-96-15 on September 17, 1996 to establish procedures for the Direct Registration System. On October 7, 1996, it amended the filing.

The amendment deleted the requirement that limited participants accept dividend-reinvestment instructions from DTC on DRS-eligible securities that offered dividend-reinvestment plans. It also deleted the requirement that, to participate in DRS, a DRS issue with a dividend-reinvestment plan had to make that plan available to street-name holders, and it modified the Limited Participant Account Agreement accordingly.

## Joint-industry origin of the DRS features

The amendment contains an unusually useful contemporaneous statement about how DRS was developed:

> Although DTC did not solicit comments on DRS, over the last two years, a joint committee of representatives of the Securities Transfer Association, the Securities Industry Association, the Corporate Transfer Agents Association, and the depositories has met and agreed on the features of DRS.

SEC-issued text, lines 104–112:

https://www.sec.gov/files/rules/sro/dtc96152.txt

This is direct evidence that the operative features of DRS were developed through a multi-constituency industry process rather than being described in the filing as a DTC-only creation.
