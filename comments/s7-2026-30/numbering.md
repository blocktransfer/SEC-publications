The system was:

1. The transfer agent sends one notice to the “appropriate qualified registered securities depository.”
2. That depository distributes or makes the information available to its participants and forwards it where required.

The SEC centralized the process because sending notices to every depository was redundant, costly, and created uncertainty about where notices should go. DTC held more than 98.8% of the securities then held by the three corporate-securities depositories. In 1995, the SEC formally designated DTC as the central recipient.
